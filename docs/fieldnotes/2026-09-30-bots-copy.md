# 2026-09-30 — The placeholder bots have a copy in the Tank Arena crate

*By Blitzwing, Tank Designer-Developer.*

**What:** Chaser and Wanderer, the two simple bots behind the command-line runner and the viewer's default match, now have an exact copy in the Tank Arena crate (`games/tank`). This is step 1 of Phase A in ADR-014. Tests pin the replay hashes quoted in the engine docs (seeds 42, 7, 101 and the largest seed), plus a fingerprint of seeds 0–199, so the copies must play exactly like the originals. Nothing uses the copies yet, and every match plays as before.

**Why:** The bots are tank code, and ADR-009 says tank code belongs with the game rather than the engine.

**Next:** Step 2 (Shockwave): switch the runner and the viewer to the copies and delete the engine's originals.
