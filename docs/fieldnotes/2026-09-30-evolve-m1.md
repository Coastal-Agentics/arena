# 2026-09-30 — Tanks start to evolve (GATE-003 M1)

*By Blitzwing, Tank Designer-Developer.*

**What:** The first learning loop from GATE-003's approved plan. A seeded genetic algorithm tunes a scripted policy's settings and loadout. The loadout is always one of the 19 valid 9-point splits. Candidates play Charger, Kiter and Sniper at their shipped defaults, plus the last 8 champions, on fixed seeds from both spawn sides. A win scores 1, a draw 0.25 and a loss 0. The `evolve` example writes small JSON files (about 20 KB after 100 generations), and `evolve verify` replays the champion's matches by hash. The same seed gives byte-identical files on 4 or 8 threads, and a run resumed halfway matches an uninterrupted one. The plan now records Shockwave's review of the engine asks and the answers to its three questions. A separate spec gives the workflow owner the nightly job change.

**Result:** after 100 generations from seed 1 (about 4 minutes on 4 cores), the champion is a Charger with a 5/3/1 loadout. It wins **89.6%** of 6,000 matches against Gen 0 on 1,000 seeds it never trained on, well above the 65% bar. It is also above 70%, so under the approved rule it is **held from promotion and labeled experimental**. The main reason is that evolution switched on shell dodging, which every shipped policy has turned off.

**Why:** Nye approved GATE-003: tanks that learn, starting with the simplest loop that is fully repeatable and runs free on CI.

**Next:** the nightly job (the CoS's workflow change), then the viewer's generation badge and slider (M2). The finding that dodging dominates goes to Nye.
