# 2026-10-11 — The Nyborg app opens: your Nyborgs at /arena/play/

*By Blitzwing /nyborg.*

**What:** milestone 1 of `docs/design/nyborg-app.md`. The Nyborg app has a home: **`/arena/play/`**.
- **My Nyborgs:** your Nyborg in the spotlight (big, bobbing, turning around), its build for each arena, and a **Race ▶** and a **Tank ▶** button. Below it, every Nyborg as a card with its own Race and Tank buttons; tap a card to make it your Nyborg. **+ New Nyborg** goes to Hatch.
- **Hatch:** a first visit goes straight to "Say hi! Hatch your first Nyborg": a name, a hairdo (all five styles, previewed on your Nyborg) and a colour, then **Hatch!**. Builds start at the balanced defaults.
- **Play:** Race puts your Nyborg on the grid with three house drivers. Tank puts it in Blue against the house tank. Both open today's viewer with your Nyborg's look and name. The links are today's link format, so a shared link plays the same match for anyone.
- **Under the hood:** one static page with hash routes (`#/`, `#/new`), plain ES modules, the same library and storage keys as the Customizer (no migration), and the doc's arena plug-in contract starting as `web/games/` (racing, tank). A game shows up when the engine lists it and it has a module; the screens never name a game.

**Verified:** headless screenshots of the first-visit Hatch, the home screen with three Nyborgs (blue mop, yellow pom, red crest), a race and a tank match opened from home with Pip named and drawn in its mop, and the home screen at 390 px with no sideways scroll. No console errors. Node tests: 25 pass, including 4 new app tests (routes, the arena registry with a fake third game, and links from home playing the same race and tank match as a direct engine run). `cargo test -q --workspace`, `check-viewer.mjs`, `check-parity.mjs` and the fieldnotes check pass.
