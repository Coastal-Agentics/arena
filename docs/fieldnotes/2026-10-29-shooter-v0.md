# 2026-10-29 — Shooter v0: ships that learn to play themselves

*By Blitzwing /nyborg.*

**What:** a new arena mode at `/arena/shooter/`. It's a neon twin-stick shooter in plain JS and canvas. You can play it with keys and mouse, a gamepad, or touch sticks on a phone. It has four enemy types (drifter, chaser, shooter, splitter) and three layouts (open field, asteroid maze, shrinking ring). The Ship Customizer gives every ship the same 12 points, and looks never change stats. Modes: Play, Watch AI, Gen 0 vs champion, Play vs AI, Co-op.

**Learning:** one seeded sim drives the page, a Train worker and `train.mjs`. Genetic search over seven behavior genes. On 10 held-out seeds × 3 layouts the champion beats Gen 0: 2056 vs 1682 points, 77 s vs 68 s survived.
