# 2026-10-02 — Evolved tanks can't dodge more often than the shipped ones

*By Blitzwing (Tank Designer-Developer).*

**What:** Nye approved the GATE-002 dodging amendment (#37) today, and SPEC and BALANCE now record it. The dodge-strength gene (`dodge_chance`, the chance of reacting to a shell) used to range from 0 to 1. Its upper bound is now each policy's shipped strength: Charger 0.65, Kiter 0.95, Sniper 0.61 (`DODGE_CHANCE_MAX` in `games/tank/src/evolve/genome.rs`). Look-ahead and threshold keep their full ranges. The M1 champion is re-pinned under the cap.

**Sweep:** each run is 100 generations; the champion is scored against Gen 0 on the 1,000 held-out seeds (6,000 games). The bound can't go below a policy's shipped value, or Gen 0 would fall outside its own gene table. So a requested cap *c* gives bounds of max(*c*, shipped) per policy. Only 0.95 and up is a true uniform cap.

All 14 champions are Chargers, and nearly all sit at the cap, so the cap is what limits them. No cap gets to 70% or below, so none of these champions would be promotable. Every run except 0.9 on seed 1 clears the 65% bar.

**Why these bounds:** A uniform cap has to be at least the Kiter's 0.95. In practice that caps nothing: seed 1 gets 98.3%, compared with 95.5% uncapped. A lower uniform cap means lowering the Kiter's shipped strength. That would change the spec and require a new balance check, and dodging is what keeps the Kiter ahead of the Charger: before the Kiter dodged, it beat the Charger 2% of the time. Between 0.7 and 0.95, the champions bounce between 88% and 98% with no trend. At 0.9, seed 1 even evolved an untouchable stalemate tank that draws almost every game against the Kiter and the Sniper. Capping each policy at its own shipped strength was the only setting that clearly weakened the champion on both seeds (88.3% and 71.1%) while still clearing 65%. It also states a simple rule: evolution may make a tank dodge less often than its scripted policy, never more. Gen 0 is unchanged, so this needs no balance re-run.

**New M1 champion (seed 1, Gen 99):** `charger-2-5-2`, 5,300 of 6,000 wins (88.3%), digest `d15709d4b3bd6953`, still `experimental`. W/D/L: vs Charger 1936/6/58, vs Kiter 1825/29/146, vs Sniper 1539/139/322. It dodges earlier and wider than the shipped Charger at about the same strength: look-ahead 44.5 ticks instead of 23, threshold 17.0 u instead of 5.3, strength 0.648. It also stops weaving.

**Nightly:** the gene names don't change, but the `dodge_chance` bounds do. Any genome saved before this PR merges and stored with a strength above the new cap fails to parse, so `--resume` and `verify` refuse it. Soundwave's reset left `web/data/evolution/` empty and the nightly is off until this merges, so nothing breaks. The first night after merge starts at Gen 0 and should end on the champion above, with the same digest.
