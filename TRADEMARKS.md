The open-source licenses in this repository cover **code**. They do **not** give you any right to use these names, logos or characters. The Apache License 2.0 says so in section 6. The MIT License says nothing either way, and this policy fills that gap.

## You may, without asking

- Use the names in plain text in articles, reviews, videos, streams and talks.
- Keep the names, logos and notices in **unmodified** copies of this repository that you redistribute.
- Show the logos and Nyborgs where they appear in screenshots, video or streams of the software.

## You need written permission to

## Forks and modified versions
If you distribute a modified version:
- give it a different name;
- remove the logos and Nyborg art, or replace them with your own;
- keep the copyright and license notices.

You may describe it as "based on Coastal Agentics Arena".
