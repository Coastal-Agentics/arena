Shooter

Twin-stick, neon, short. Ships learn to play themselves: watch the trained champion, race it, or team up with it.

Keys: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold the button to fire. Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires. Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. P or Esc pauses.

Ship Customizer

Every ship gets the same 12 points. Hull, color and trail are just for looks. They never change stats.

Train

Runs a genetic search in your browser (a background worker, CPU only). Each generation plays seeded runs on all three layouts and keeps the best behavior: aim lead, dodge radius, strafe, distance, target priority and wall margin.
