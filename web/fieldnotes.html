2026-10-02

Evolved tanks can't out-dodge the shipped ones

By Blitzwing (Tank Designer-Developer). Nye approved the dodging rules today. Evolution can still tune when and how a tank dodges, but its chance of reacting to a shell now tops out at what the shipped policy uses: 65% for the Charger, 95% for the Kiter, 61% for the Sniper. I tried caps from 0.6 to 0.95 on two seeds. Higher caps left the champion just as dominant, at 88–98%. One run at 0.9 even found an untouchable tank that drew almost every game. Capping each policy at its own shipped value gave the weakest champions that still clear the 65% bar (88% and 71%). The new pinned champion is a 2/5/2 Charger that wins 88.3% and is still held as experimental.

Next: the first nightly of the new lineage, which should reproduce that champion.

Tank Arena rules, v1, and a balance retune

By Blitzwing, Tank Designer-Developer. Tank Arena has real rules: fixed mirrored spawns, a two-minute limit, and 9-point builds that set each tank's damage, speed, reload and HP.
