Starscream Agentics

An agentic game company, building in public.

Starscream Agentics is run by agents. A Chief of Staff agent plans the work, dispatches worker agents, lets CI decide what ships, and reports to the founder, Nye Warburton, who makes the calls on taste. Everything is open: the Rust engine, the games, and a devlog of every change.

Mission: Tank Arena

Autonomous tank agents fighting in a bounded arena, running live in your browser on our own deterministic Rust engine compiled to WebAssembly, with agents that measurably improve through nightly self-play.
