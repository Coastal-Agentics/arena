# TBD MuJoCo model v0.1: parameters

_Internal. **Physics sim with estimated parameters.** Nothing below has been measured on hardware. TBD is the working name._

The model is `tbd.xml`, generated by `gen_mjcf.py`. Meshes come from `build_meshes.py`, which reads the CAD v0.2 deployed GLB. The flight controller and battery model live in `tbd_control.py`, mirrored in `web/tbd_control.js`.

## Frame and layout (from CAD v0.2, `cad/tbd_geom_v02.py`)
- **World and body frame:** +X forward (face), +Y left, +Z up. The body origin sits at floor level under the body centre.
- **Wheels:** 4 wheels at (±66, ±38, 22) mm, radius 22 mm, width 14 mm, all driven (skid steer).
- **Fold hinges:** 4 hinges at (±12.5, ±72.5, 37) mm, axis parallel to X.
- **Fold angle:** 0 = deployed (flight), 90° = stowed (ground). Range −1° to 91°.
- **Duct centres when deployed:** (±55, ±111.5) mm. That's 223 mm side to side and 110 mm fore-aft. Duct OD is 87 mm and height is 29 mm.
- **Thrust sites:** at each duct centre, with force along the duct axis (site +Z). Thrust follows the duct as it folds, so thrust while folded pushes sideways.

## Power and battery (controller-side model, not in the MJCF)
- **Pack:** Tattu 6S 850 mAh, 18.9 Wh. We count 80% as usable, which gives 15.1 Wh.
- **Pack cells:** 6S. Open-circuit voltage runs linearly from 4.2 V to 3.5 V per cell as the full pack goes from full to empty. 3.5 V/cell is a landing-voltage end point ([Oscar Liang](https://oscarliang.com/monitor-measure-battery-voltage-alarm-drone/)). Pack: [Tattu R-Line 850 mAh 6S](https://genstattu.com/tattu-r-line-version-5-0-850mah-6s-22-2v-150c-lipo-battery-pack-with-xt30u-f-plug/).
- **Hover efficiency:** **2.3 gf/W placeholder, range 1.8–2.5. ESTIMATE.** The EMAX 6S figure used in the BOM sits in that band; it was not measured on the ducted D76.
- **Hover power:** 422 W for the props plus 6 W for avionics (est.), about **428 W** in total. At mid-pack that is about **19 A**.
- **Off-hover power:** each duct draws P_hover × (T/T_hover)^1.5, from momentum theory scaling.
- **Driving:** about 1 W per wheel motor while driving (est.).
- **Estimated hover time:** **~2.1 min** at 2.3 gf/W, or ~2.8 min at 3.0 gf/W. This matches the BOM's "~2 minutes".
- **Voltage sag (game-side, default on, `voltage_sag`):** `V = V_oc(soc) − I R`, then thrust scales with `(V / 25.2 V)²`. `R` defaults to **7.5 mΩ per cell** (pack 45 mΩ), plausible band **5–10 mΩ**. **ESTIMATE, unsourced; fit from a logged flight or measure with a charger IR readout.** Switch it off with `"voltage_sag": false`. `r_cell_ohm` overrides the per-cell value (ohms).

## Fold mechanism
- **Servo:** one ES08MD II. It drives the carriage and four push rods to the four arms.
- **Coupling:** in MuJoCo the arms are coupled by three `joint` equality constraints to `fold_FL`, which is the rigid linkage. One `position` actuator acts on `fold_FL`.
- **Actuator:** kp 6, kv 0.15, force limit ±2.5 N·m at the arm. The stiff hold **also stands in for the spring lock pins**. Without them, thrust torque (about 0.2 N·m per arm) would fold the arms up in flight. In the sim the arms lift about 2.6° under hover thrust.
- **Fold time:** the MuJoCo position actuator alone still reaches the stop in about 0.11 s (kp 6, force limit ±2.5 N·m). That is faster than the servo. The game now slews the fold **command** (`fold_rate_limit`, default on) so a full 90° takes about **0.19 s**, about **465°/s** at the arm. Source: [ES08MD II](https://www.rotorama.com/assets/docs/394dce6cabeb549fc55e8793485c6253/48060-1/emax-es08md-ii-manual-en.pdf), 0.10 s per 60° with no load at 6 V; ±58° of horn travel gives about 0.19 s. **No-load figure.** The rate limit stays in the game command rather than in `tbd.xml`: a position actuator has no rate attribute, and the stiff `kp` is what holds the arms against thrust. `"fold_rate_limit": false` restores the instant command.

## Collision geometry
Collision uses simple primitives, kept separate from the visual meshes (visual meshes use contype 0, group 2):
- body: a box (204×110×94 mm) plus an ellipsoid dome
- each duct: a cylinder (r 43.5, h 29 mm)
- each tyre: a cylinder (r 22, w 14 mm)

## Test scene
- Floor: 12 × 12 m.
- Staircase at x = 0.80 m: **4 risers of 17 cm** with 28 cm treads, 1.0 m wide, leading to a 1.2 m deep landing at 0.68 m.

## Controller (hand-tuned, simple)
- **Position and velocity:** PD (kp 5, kd 4), output capped at 4 m/s². That gives a desired tilt, capped at 23°.
- **Altitude:** PID (8, 5, 2).
- **Attitude:** PD at ωn 14 rad/s, ζ 0.9, using body rates.
- **Yaw:** PD.
- **Mixer:** analytic X mixer. It doesn't model saturation priority.

## Ram drag (game-side, default on, `ram_drag`)

Per duct, in forward flight: `F ≈ ṁ V_air` opposite the in-plane airspeed, with `ṁ = T / v_i` and `v_i = √(T / (2 ρ A))`. `A = π (0.038)²`, the Gemfan D76 disk ([product page](https://www.gemfanhobby.com/d76-ducted-pc-5-blade-15mmm5.html)). At hover thrust and 4 m/s this is about **2–3 N** total. The ducts sit below the body centre of mass, so the force has a small pitch moment. The game reports `aero_force_n` and `aero_torque_nm` in the world frame, torque about the body COM, for `xfrc_applied` on body `tbd`. **ESTIMATE.** `"ram_drag": false` zeros both.

## Ground effect (game-side, default on, `ground_effect`)

`T_IGE / T_OGE = 1 / (1 − (R / 4h)²)` with `R = 0.038` m. `h` is the duct-plane height: `range_down` plus 23 mm (rangefinder site at 14 mm, duct plane at 37 mm), and `h` is clamped to at least `R/2`. About **+7%** at liftoff (37 mm) and about **1.00** at 1 m. Source: Cheeseman and Bennett, ARC R&M 3021, which assumes an **open rotor**, so this is an **ESTIMATE** for a duct. [ARC R&M 3021](https://reports.aerade.cranfield.ac.uk/bitstream/handle/1826.2/3590/arc-rm-3021.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1). `"ground_effect": false` leaves the ratio at 1.

- motor or ESC thermal limits
- sensor noise or state estimation (the controller reads true state)
- servo backlash and load slowdown (the fold rate is the no-load figure)
- lock-pin engagement logic
- crash damage
