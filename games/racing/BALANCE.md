## 6. Values: first cut → tuned

Hand-maintained text, printed by the example. Physics is unchanged from R1.

| Value | R2 first cut | Tuned (shipped) |
|---|---|---|
| Braking (u/s²) | 480 | 480 (kept) |
| Follower look-ahead / corner speed / braking distance | 70 u / 160 u/s / 20 u | 70 u / 163 u/s / 20 u |
| Cutter apex margin / entry speed / exit point | 46 u / 128 u/s / 90 u | 46 u / 128 u/s / 90 u (kept) |
| Cutter in traffic | passes like the Follower, half the offset | lifts behind a car within 40 u (or alongside); passes only with 130 u of straight |
| Blocker corner speed / block distance / block strength | 160 u/s / 120 u / 1.0 | 164 u/s / 70 u / 0.8 |

Why: with the first-cut values the Cutter won 93% of mixed races (2,242 of 2,400) despite a solo edge of about 1%: it took the lead from any slot in the first corners by diving inside, and the others never got it back. Making it patient in traffic brought that to the table above. The Blocker's blocking costs it more time than it gains (it wins fewer mixed races with blocking on than off), so the block was shortened and softened and its corner speed raised; it is the weakest baseline in traffic, not the strongest as racing.md intends. That's a known gap for R4's evolution, not an acceptance target.
